# English platforms

These are editorial choices, not claims about an algorithm or current technical limits. Use the confirmed speaker, objective, audience, evidence and authorized voice. Check current platform/account limits when a deliverable depends on them. Do not route other platforms through these formats.

## Instagram

Confirm the requested format when it changes the deliverable:

- **Caption:** write the caption and relevant CTA/tags only when useful; describe an assumed asset rather than pretending it exists.
- **Carousel:** provide cover text and ordered page copy, with each page's purpose, visual hierarchy and asset note. End with a relevant action if the task calls for one, not a compulsory engagement question. The page plan is not a finished image set.
- **Reel:** provide concept, opening visual/action, spoken script, shots, on-screen copy/subtitles and caption. Note which clips/assets are needed; do not claim to have filmed or rendered it.

A retailer may describe the product they carry, but cannot invent having manufactured it. A personal recommendation may use first person only for real supplied experience. Brand ownership or commercial relationships remain clear. Avoid invented social proof or universal claims about saves/reach. No fixed slide count, length or hashtag quota is imposed.

## TikTok

Deliver a producible short-video plan, not just a caption. Use a compact table of **beat/approximate timing, shot/action, spoken line, on-screen text/subtitle, and asset required**, followed by a caption. Timing is an editorial estimate and can be revised to the user's production constraints; do not call it an algorithm optimum.

Open with the concrete subject or question; choose demo, explanation or story only when the assets/evidence support it. Keep product claims identical across voiceover, subtitles and caption. A retailer's demo must not turn into a fake consumer reaction or invented long-term use. Mark stock/illustrative/generative assets as such. Do not assume music/trend rights, invent a trending sound or promise finished clips when only writing was requested.

## X

Choose a single post when one complete point fits; use a thread when the reasoning needs connected parts. Supply standalone opening context and keep attribution/qualifiers in the same part as the claim. For a thread, number or label parts for editing and avoid repetitive hooks. If strict limits matter, check the actual account/format limits or label length validation pending; do not assume all accounts have the same allowance.

Use a brand/retailer voice for commercial statements and an expert's real judgment for a professional viewpoint. Do not invent a controversial position, statistic, urgency or consensus to provoke replies. Links and tags follow the task and current rules, not an unsupported “always hide links” tactic.

## Facebook

Identify whether the post is for a page or a particular group. Page copy can carry relevant product/service facts and a clear business action, with dates, location and offer terms only if supplied and current. A group post must fit the group's purpose and promotion rules; without those rules, return a draft awaiting the check.

Write enough context for the intended local or interest community. Do not pretend that a page owner, retailer or commercial partner is an unrelated member. Group membership, moderation approval, event creation and actual video production are not performed by this skill.

## Reddit

Ask for the target subreddit/community and actual post type. Read supplied current rules or use permitted tools to inspect them; check self-promotion, affiliation, links, flair and format requirements that affect the draft. If the rules are unavailable, mark the draft **COMMUNITY RULES NOT CHECKED**, and avoid claiming it is ready to post. Do not invent moderator approval.

Write a useful standalone discussion, explanation or answer suited to the actual community. State a relevant brand, retail, employment or sponsorship relationship plainly. Do not pose as a satisfied customer, fabricate a purchase story, hide a commercial link or stage “organic” endorsements. If promotion is disallowed, recommend a compliant informational reframing or hold the draft; do not advise evasion. No posting, vote manipulation, mass commenting or DMs.
